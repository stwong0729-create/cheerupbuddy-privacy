CheerUp Buddy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Introduction

CheerUp Buddy (CUB) is an AI-powered happiness companion offering supportive conversations, encouragement, jokes, goal-related guidance, and optional voice interactions.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, share, and protect information when you use CheerUp Buddy.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use CUB, we may collect and process:

  • Account Information: Your email address, name, and authentication information associated with your account.
  • Preferences: Your selected buddy voice, motivation style, interests, and personalization settings.
  • Goals: Personal goals and related information you choose to share.
  • Chat Information: Messages, relevant conversation history, and happiness session content.
  • Voice Information: Audio recordings or speech transcripts processed when you choose to use voice features.
  • Technical Information: Device, diagnostic, and usage information needed to operate, secure, and improve the app.
  • Subscription Information: Subscription status and related transaction information provided by the applicable payment platform.

Please share only information you are comfortable providing.

How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Provide AI-powered conversations and encouragement.
  • Generate jokes and goal-related guidance.
  • Personalize your CUB experience.
  • Process optional voice interactions and speech transcription.
  • Generate or play spoken responses.
  • Maintain accounts and subscription access.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain app security and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Understand app usage and improve our services.

AI Service Providers

CheerUp Buddy uses OpenAI, a third-party AI service provider, to support AI-generated conversations, encouragement, goal-related guidance, speech processing, and other AI-powered features.

Depending on the feature requested, relevant chat messages, conversation history, goals, preferences, voice recordings, or speech transcripts may be transmitted to OpenAI to process the request and generate a response.

We share information with AI service providers for the purpose of providing the requested AI functionality.

AI Consent and Your Choices

Before you use CUB's AI-powered features, we ask for your explicit permission to transmit relevant information to our third-party AI service provider.

You can choose whether to allow this processing.

Agree & Continue: By selecting this option, you consent to CUB transmitting relevant information to its AI service provider to deliver the AI-powered features you request.

Not Now: If you do not agree, CUB's AI-powered features will not be available. You may review the consent information and reconsider your decision later.

Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your AI consent through the AI Privacy & Consent section of your Profile. Withdrawing consent stops future AI requests that rely on your consent.

Withdrawal does not automatically delete information previously processed or stored. You may separately request deletion of your personal information.

Microphone and Voice Privacy

Microphone access is optional. CUB requests microphone permission when you choose to use a feature requiring your microphone.

When you use voice features, audio recordings or speech transcripts may be transmitted to third-party AI services for transcription, processing, or response generation.

You can decline microphone permission and continue using text-based AI features if you have granted AI processing consent and have the required app access.

You may also manage microphone permissions through your device settings.

Guest Access and Free Cheer-Ups

CUB offers a guest experience that allows eligible users to try five free AI interactions, called Cheer-Ups, without purchasing a subscription.

Guest users must agree to AI processing before using these AI-powered features.

After the five free Cheer-Ups are used, a subscription may be required to continue accessing AI-powered features. Registered users may also need an active subscription before accessing these features.

Account Access and Authentication

CUB may allow users to create accounts or sign in using email, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In.

Authentication information may be processed by the app's authentication providers to support secure account access.

Guest access may use an automatically created account to maintain guest usage and preferences.

Data Retention

Account information, chat messages, session content, preferences, and goals may be retained while your account remains active, unless you delete them or request deletion.

Voice recordings may be processed temporarily to provide speech-related functionality. Retention of audio and transcripts depends on the services and features involved.

Withdrawing AI consent prevents future AI processing that depends on consent but does not automatically erase previously stored information.

Some information may be retained where necessary for legal, security, fraud prevention, or operational requirements. Third-party providers may have their own applicable retention practices.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit and access controls, designed to protect information handled by CheerUp Buddy.

No internet transmission or electronic storage system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Subscriptions and Payments

CheerUp Buddy offers subscriptions through the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Subscription payments and billing are processed by Apple or Google, depending on the platform used. CheerUp Buddy does not receive your full payment card details.

You can manage or cancel your subscription through the applicable app store. Subscription billing and payment information are subject to Apple's or Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

CheerUp Buddy is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us so we can investigate and arrange appropriate deletion.

Your Privacy Rights and Data Deletion

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

You may use available app controls to update information, withdraw AI consent, or request account deletion.

You may also contact us directly to request deletion or ask questions about how your information is handled. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

To learn more about account deletion, visit: Account Deletion Information .

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our app, features, service providers, or privacy practices change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
