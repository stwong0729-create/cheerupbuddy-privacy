Delete Your CheerUp Buddy Account

CheerUp Buddy users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Delete your account in the app

Open CheerUp Buddy, go to Profile, and select Delete my account.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the app, email stephenw@minddora.com using the email address associated with your CheerUp Buddy account.

Please use the subject line: CheerUp Buddy Account Deletion Request.

Data deleted

When your account is deleted, CheerUp Buddy will delete your account information, preferences, goals, chat history, voice-related data stored with your account, and other associated user data, except information that must be retained when required by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or transaction record purposes.

Deletion timeframe

Account deletion requests will be processed within 30 days.
